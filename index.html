Who We Are

Dike John Reservoir Tech Limited (also trading as Dike John Subsurface Engineering Co) is a specialized oil and gas engineering firm based in Lagos, Nigeria. Under the leadership of our Drilling Superintendent, we bring decades of combined experience in subsurface engineering, drilling operations, and reservoir management.

Our expertise spans the complete lifecycle of oil and gas projects, from initial exploration and appraisal through to production optimization. We understand the unique challenges of operating in Nigeria's dynamic energy sector and provide solutions that balance technical precision with operational efficiency.

Committed to safety, innovation, and environmental responsibility, we work closely with operators, contractors, and regulatory bodies to deliver projects that meet international standards while supporting Nigeria's energy independence goals.

Our Services

Comprehensive Subsurface Engineering Solutions

Drilling Operations Management

Complete drilling superintendent services including rig supervision, drilling program development, well planning, and real-time operational oversight. We ensure safe, efficient drilling operations that meet technical specifications and regulatory requirements.

Reservoir Engineering & Management

Advanced reservoir characterization, performance analysis, and production optimization strategies. We utilize cutting-edge technology and proven methodologies to maximize recovery and extend field life.

Subsurface Technical Consulting

Project Management & Coordination

Operational Safety & Compliance
